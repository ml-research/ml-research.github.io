Why Probabilistic Graphical Models?

PGMs combine graph theory and probability theory to model complex collections of random variables in a structured way. They are central to machine learning, robotics, computer vision, NLP, and bioinformatics.

The course covers formal foundations, modeling principles, exact and approximate inference, and parameter and structure learning. This is complemented by applications, current research examples, and an outlook on generative models.

Bayesian Networks

Independencies, factorization, d-separation, and variable elimination.

Learning

Maximum likelihood, EM, Bayesian parameter estimation, and structure learning.

Temporal Models

Hidden Markov models, dynamic Bayesian networks, and recursive filters.

Tractable & Deep PGMs

Sum-product networks, probabilistic circuits, and deep generative models.
